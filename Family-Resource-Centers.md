# Family Resource Centers

Neighborhood family resource centers offering counseling, parenting classes, benefit application help, and referrals.

**FaCT network check (2026-10-04):** The current Families and Communities Together list at https://www.factoc.org/locations includes Anaheim Independencia, Manzanita Park (Anaheim), Friendly Center Buena Park, Magnolia Park, Oak View, South Orange County, Newport Mesa, El Modena, CHEC (San Juan Capistrano), Corbin, Downtown (Santa Ana), Minnie Street, Stanton, Tustin, and Westminster. Centers below marked "not on the current FaCT list" may have closed or changed. FaCT main line: 714-566-2878, info@factoc.org.

**Status:** copied from source lists dated 2026. Not yet checked with each organization.

> [!important] Call before you go
> Programs, phone numbers, and eligibility rules change. Call the organization or check its website before visiting. For help finding any service in Orange County, dial **2-1-1** (24/7, multilingual).
