## Applying for Benefits

### BenefitsCal
- **What they do:** California's online portal to apply for, manage, and renew CalFresh, CalWORKs, Medi-Cal, and other public benefits. You can upload documents and check case status online.
- **Who it serves:** Orange County residents
- **Website:** https://benefitscal.com/
- **Source:** RCOC community resources spreadsheet (Community Resources 2026)

- **What they do:** Benefits information for major public benefits. Real world knowledge about work, disability, healthcare and other benefits.
- **Who it serves:** Public, private and non profit direct service organizations or anyone living with a disability

## In-Home Help

### In-Home Supportive Services (IHSS)
- **What they do:** In-Home Supportive Services (IHSS) provide people with low income who are blind, disabled, or 65 years old or older with personal assistance and in-home services so they can safely stay in their homes or continue working. Services range from assistance with household chores to personal care and paramedical services.
- **Who it serves:** Low income aged, blind and disabled persons, living in on their own or family home.
- **Phone:** 714-825-3000
- **Website:** https://www.ssa.ocgov.com/elder/ihss/
- **Source:** RCOC community resources spreadsheet (Community Resources 2026)

## Social Security

### Social Security Administration (SSA)
- **What they do:** Some services include: providing an Earnings and Benefit Statement record for use in estimating retirement benefits, Medicare sign- ups and benefit information. Provides financial benefit to some disabled individuals.
- **Who it serves:** Eligibility varies by program. Earned financial benefit for persons who have paid into Social Security the required number of quarters.
- **Phone:** 800-772-1213
- **Website:** https://www.ssa.gov
- **Source:** RCOC community resources spreadsheet (Community Resources 2026)

### Social Security Disability Starter Kit (website)
- **What they do:** A tool designed to help individuals prepare to participate in a disability interview or complete an on-line application for SSI benefits. The kit contains a fact sheet about applying, a checklist of documents and information that SSA will request and a worksheet to assist applicant
- **Who it serves:** For persons with disabilities wishing to apply for Social Security disability benefits.
- **Website:** https://www.ssa.gov/disability/disability_starter_kits_adult_eng.htm
- **Source:** RCOC community resources spreadsheet (Community Resources 2026)

### Supplemental Security Income (SSI)
- **What they do:** Provides a monthly financial benefit and Medi-Cal benefits to people who are eligible. It is an unearned financial benefit.
- **Who it serves:** People age 65+ years, blind or disabled with low income and limited assets.
- **Phone:** 800-772-1213 (TTY 800-325-0778)
- **Website:** https://www.ssa.gov
- **Notes:** Administered by the Social Security Administration.
- **Source:** RCOC community resources spreadsheet (Community Resources 2026)

## Bill Discounts

### Southern California Edison CARE Program
- **What they do:** Monthly discount on electric bills for income-qualified households.
- **Who it serves:** Households where a member receives SSI, CalFresh/SNAP, WIC, or Medi-Cal may qualify.
- **Website:** https://www.sce.com/residential/income-qualified/CAREFERA/
- **Source:** RCOC community resources spreadsheet (Community Resources 2026)

## Nutrition

### WIC (Women, Infants & Children) – Orange County
- **What they do:** Provides nutrition education, supplemental food, access to health care and other social services and breastfeeding support. Services are provided through a network of clinics located throughout Orange County.
- **Who it serves:** Women who are low to moderate income, pregnant, breastfeeding and post-partum and children up to five years of age who are at nutritional risk. Medical referral required. Serves Orange County residents.
- **Phone:** 888-942-2229
- **Website:** https://www.phfewic.org
- **Notes:** WIC benefits do not cover diapers, but some WIC offices hand out CAP OC Diaper Bank diapers to their clients. See [[Diaper-Resources]].
- **Source:** RCOC community resources spreadsheet (Community Resources 2026)

## Money and Savings

### Free Tax Help (VITA – Volunteer Income Tax Assistance)
- **What they do:** Free basic tax return preparation and e-filing by IRS-certified volunteers for people who generally make about $70,000 or less, people with disabilities, and limited-English speakers. Tax Counseling for the Elderly (TCE) helps people 60 and older.
- **Who it serves:** Low income persons and the individuals that we serve.
- **Phone:** 800-906-9887
- **Website:** https://www.irs.gov/individuals/free-tax-return-preparation-for-qualifying-taxpayers
- **Notes:** The IRS says VITA generally serves people who make about $70,244 or less (2026), but there is no program-wide limit and each site may set its own. The source listed an older $54,000 figure. Call to find a nearby site (checked 2026-10-05).
- **Source:** RCOC community resources spreadsheet (Community Resources 2026)

### CalABLE
- **What they do:** California's tax-advantaged savings program for people with disabilities. Lets people save without losing SSI or Medi-Cal eligibility, with earnings growing tax-free when spent on qualified disability expenses.
- **Who it serves:** Disability must have begun before age 46 (raised from age 26, effective January 1, 2026). Must either receive SSI or SSDI based on that disability, or self-certify with a signed diagnosis from a licensed physician.
- **Phone:** 833-225-2253 (fax 844-761-0239)
- **Website:** https://calable.ca.gov
- **Notes:** Confirmed on calable.ca.gov (2026-10-05): standard annual contribution limit is **$20,000 for 2026**. People who work may be able to add up to $15,650 more under ABLE to Work. The first $100,000 in the account does not count against SSI.
- **Source:** RCOC community resources spreadsheet (Community Resources 2026)

### Jewish Free Loan Association
- **What they do:** Interest-free loans for education, health care, business, and green home improvements.
- **Who it serves:** Families of children with special needs of all faiths.
- **Phone:** 323-761-8830
- **Email:** info@jfla.org
- **Website:** https://www.jfla.org
- **Notes:** Based in Los Angeles. Interest-free loans of up to $5,000 for students, $10,000 for health care, and $15,000 for business; loans also for green home improvements.
- **Source:** RCOC community resources spreadsheet (Community Resources 2026)
