# Disability and Developmental Services

Regional Center, assistive technology, diagnosis-specific organizations, and disability support.

**Status:** copied from source lists dated 2026. Not yet checked with each organization.

> [!important] Call before you go
> Programs, phone numbers, and eligibility rules change. Call the organization or check its website before visiting. For help finding any service in Orange County, dial **2-1-1** (24/7, multilingual).

## In This File

- Independent Living: Dayle McIntosh Center (DMC)
- Assistive Technology: ATEC (Assistive Technology Exchange Center) – Goodwill of Orange County
- Equipment and Accommodations: California Telephone Access Program (CTAP), Disabled Person Parking Placard (DMV)
- Diagnosis-Specific Organizations: Braille Institute – Anaheim, Center For Autism & Neurodevelopmental Disorders, Down Syndrome Association of Orange County, UCI Down Syndrome Program (UCI MIND), Epilepsy Support Network, National Organization for Rare Disorders (NORD), Sensory Impaired Guidance Network (formerly Orange County Deaf Advocacy Center), UCI Adult Primary Care Down Syndrome Clinic
- Regional Center: Regional Center of Orange County (RCOC)

## Independent Living

### Dayle McIntosh Center (DMC)
- **What they do:** An independent living center providing referrals for employment, housing and other services to adults with disabilities. DMC serves as an ombudsman for the rights of individuals with disabilities.
- **Who it serves:** Orange County residents with disabilities.
- **Address:** 501 N. Brookhurst Street, #102, Anaheim, CA 92801
- **Phone:** 714-621-3300
- **Website:** https://www.daylemc.org
- **Source:** RCOC community resources spreadsheet (Community Resources 2026)

## Assistive Technology

### ATEC (Assistive Technology Exchange Center) – Goodwill of Orange County
- **What they do:** A program of Goodwill Industries offering Assistive Technology and Augmentative Alternative Communication (AAC) consultation and assessment services, equipment trial, equipment loan and reuse and support in learning how to use assistive technology equipment.
- **Who it serves:** Serves Orange and surrounding counties. Cost of services and equipment vary by type of service/equipment.
- **Address:** 1601 E. Saint Andrew Place, Santa Ana, CA 92705
- **Phone:** 714-361-6200
- **Website:** https://www.ocgoodwill.org/programs
- **Source:** RCOC community resources spreadsheet (Community Resources 2026)

## Equipment and Accommodations

### California Telephone Access Program (CTAP)
- **What they do:** Provides equipment and training for equipment usage to eligible persons. Applications and information packets are available at CTAP.
- **Who it serves:** Eligible individuals must have a qualifying disability such as deaf/ hard of hearing, blind/low vision, deaf/blind, speech impairment, mobility/ manipulation impairment or a cognitive impairment.
- **Address:** Orange Service Center, 681 S. Parker St., #210, Orange, CA 92868
- **Phone:** 800-806-1191
- **Website:** https://www.californiaphones.org
- **Source:** RCOC community resources spreadsheet (Community Resources 2026)

### Disabled Person Parking Placard (DMV)
- **What they do:** Placard allows use of handicapped parking spaces.
- **Who it serves:** Those with impaired mobility in one or both lower extremities, both hands, visual problems or require the aid of assistive device.
- **Phone:** DMV: 800-777-0133
- **Website:** https://www.dmv.ca.gov/portal/dpp-application/
- **Notes:** Apply online or with form REG 195, signed by your medical provider. Checked on the DMV website (2026-10-05).
- **Source:** RCOC community resources spreadsheet (Community Resources 2026)

## Diagnosis-Specific Organizations

### Braille Institute – Anaheim
- **What they do:** Provides an array of free services and programs, including classes such as orientation and mobility, independent living skills, library services, a Vistas shop, Youth Programs and other benefits.
- **Who it serves:** People of all ages living with vision impairments.
- **Address:** 527 N. Dale Ave., Anaheim, CA 92801
- **Phone:** 714-821-5000
- **Website:** https://www.brailleinstitute.org/anaheim
- **Source:** RCOC community resources spreadsheet (Community Resources 2026)

### Center For Autism & Neurodevelopmental Disorders
- **What they do:** Developmental assessments, treatment for children birth to 22 years of age and educational opportunities for parents and professionals.
- **Who it serves:** Call for additional information.
- **Address:** 2500 Red Hill Ave., Santa Ana, CA 92705
- **Phone:** 949-267-0400
- **Website:** https://www.thecenter4autism.org
- **Source:** RCOC community resources spreadsheet (Community Resources 2026)

### Down Syndrome Association of Orange County
- **What they do:** Information and resources for persons with Down syndrome and their families.
- **Who it serves:** Anyone is welcome to contact the association for information and resources.
- **Address:** 151 Kalmus Dr., Ste. M-5, Costa Mesa, CA 92626
- **Phone:** 714-540-5794
- **Website:** https://www.dsaoc.org
- **Source:** RCOC community resources spreadsheet (Community Resources 2026)

### UCI Down Syndrome Program (UCI MIND)
- **What they do:** Down syndrome neurology care and research at UC Irvine, including long-term studies on brain aging and dementia in Down syndrome.
- **Who it serves:** Patients of all ages diagnosed with Down syndrome.
- **Address:** UCI Medical Center, 101 The City Drive South, Orange, CA 92868
- **Phone:** 714-456-8443
- **Website:** https://mind.uci.edu/research-studies/down-syndrome-program/
- **Notes:** Research and evaluation visits may be at UCI Medical Center in Orange or on the main UC Irvine campus (for example, Gottschalk Medical Plaza or Hewitt Hall). Call the program to confirm where your visit is. For adult primary care, see the UCI Adult Primary Care Down Syndrome Clinic below.
- **Source:** RCOC community resources spreadsheet (Community Resources 2026)

### Epilepsy Support Network
- **What they do:** Staff and parent training on seizure disorders, offers support groups for family members whose children have epilepsy
- **Who it serves:** Individuals and families touched by epilepsy.
- **Address:** 1500 Adams Ave., #301, Costa Mesa, CA 92626
- **Phone:** 714-916-0456
- **Website:** https://www.esnoc.org
- **Source:** RCOC community resources spreadsheet (Community Resources 2026)

### National Organization for Rare Disorders (NORD)
- **What they do:** Federation of voluntary health organizations and individuals dedicated to helping people with rare or "orphan" diseases and assisting the organizations that serve them.
- **Who it serves:** Services for individuals of all ages depending on the program. Anyone can request information.
- **Address:** 1779 Massachusetts Ave., #500, Washington, DC 20036
- **Phone:** 202-588-5700
- **Website:** https://www.rarediseases.org
- **Notes:** National organization based in Washington, DC.
- **Source:** RCOC community resources spreadsheet (Community Resources 2026)

### Sensory Impaired Guidance Network (formerly Orange County Deaf Advocacy Center)
- **What they do:** Provide education, tools & resources to help clients move towards independence. Please note that an appointment is REQUIRED before visiting the office.
- **Who it serves:** Services for deaf, hard of hearing, blind, low vision and individuals with autism.
- **Address:** Mailing: 2255 W. Ball Road, #2430, Anaheim, CA 92814
- **Phone:** 714-699-3323 (voice & text)
- **Website:** https://deafadvocacy.org
- **Notes:** Renamed from Orange County Deaf Advocacy Center (checked 2026-10-05). Appointment required before visiting. The Ball Road address with #2430 and ZIP 92814 appears to be a mailing address (P.O. Box 2430), not a walk-in office; call for the visit location.
- **Source:** RCOC community resources spreadsheet (Community Resources 2026)

### UCI Adult Primary Care Down Syndrome Clinic

- **Address:** UCI Family Health Center, 800 N. Main St., Santa Ana, CA 92701
- **Phone:** 657-282-6355
- **Website:** https://www.dsaoc.org/uci-adult-primary-care-down-syndrome-clinic.html
- **Source:** Down Syndrome Association of Orange County website (checked 2026-10-05)

## Regional Center

### Regional Center of Orange County (RCOC)
- **What they do:** Provides assessment, case management, service coordination and purchase of services such as residential placement, independent living skills training, vocational programs and transportation.
- **Who it serves:** Services for individuals of all ages living with a developmental disability and infants at risk for developmental disability.
- **Phone:** 714-796-5100; intake 714-796-5354
- **Website:** https://www.rcocdd.com
- **Notes:** See also Comfort Connection Family Resource Center under [[Family-Resource-Centers]].
- **Source:** RCOC community resources spreadsheet (Community Resources 2026)
