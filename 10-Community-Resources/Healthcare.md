# Healthcare

Health insurance, clinics, dental, vision, and specialty care.

**Status:** copied from source lists dated 2026. Not yet checked with each organization.

> [!important] Call before you go
> Programs, phone numbers, and eligibility rules change. Call the organization or check its website before visiting. For help finding any service in Orange County, dial **2-1-1** (24/7, multilingual).

## Health Insurance

### CalOptima
- **What they do:** Medi-Cal managed health care program for Orange County residents.
- **Who it serves:** Medi-Cal beneficiaries in Orange County.
- **Address:** 505 City Parkway West, Orange, CA 92868
- **Phone:** 714-246-8400; 888-587-8088 (TTY 800-735-2929)
- **Website:** https://www.caloptima.org
- **Source:** RCOC community resources spreadsheet (Community Resources 2026)

### CalOptima Community Liaison for RCOC
- **What they do:** Consultation and assistance for person served by RCOC in navigating the CalOptima system
- **Who it serves:** Individuals served by RCOC and members of CalOptima.
- **Phone:** 714-246-8694
- **Notes:** Direct liaison line. The source lists a staff member's name and personal email; left out here. If the line changes, call CalOptima's main number and ask for the RCOC liaison.
- **Source:** RCOC community resources spreadsheet (Community Resources 2026)

### Medi-Cal
- **What they do:** Routine and emergency medical care, lab tests, hospitalization, medical products, prescription pharmaceuticals and other medically necessary items or care. In-home private duty nursing for medically fragile children and adults.
- **Who it serves:** California residents with low income, disabilities and the elderly.
- **Phone:** 800-281-9799
- **Website:** https://www.dhcs.ca.gov
- **Notes:** Source listed medi-cal.ca.gov, an older address. In Orange County, Medi-Cal is managed through CalOptima. Apply through BenefitsCal.
- **Source:** RCOC community resources spreadsheet (Community Resources 2026)

### Medicare
- **What they do:** Provides individuals with medical insurance through the Medicare program.
- **Who it serves:** Aged 65 or older, people with disabilities – based on a work record.
- **Phone:** 800-633-4227
- **Website:** https://www.medicare.gov
- **Source:** RCOC community resources spreadsheet (Community Resources 2026)

## Finding Care

### Coalition of Orange County Health Centers
- **What they do:** Assistance finding health care for uninsured, Medi-Cal beneficiaries and application assistance for Covered California.
- **Who it serves:** For children of families with no medical insurance and limited financial resources
- **Phone:** 714-352-5990
- **Website:** https://coalitionoc.org
- **Notes:** Source listed coccc.org; the coalition's current site is coalitionoc.org (2026-10-04).
- **Source:** RCOC community resources spreadsheet (Community Resources 2026)

### National Alliance for Hispanic Health Helpline
- **What they do:** A bilingual, toll-free service designed to help Hispanic families connect with basic health information, local health providers and federally supported programs, including the State Children's Health Insurance Program.
- **Who it serves:** Designed for Hispanic and Spanish speaking families.
- **Phone:** 866-783-2645
- **Website:** https://www.hispanichealth.org
- **Source:** RCOC community resources spreadsheet (Community Resources 2026)

### Orange County Medical Association (website)
- **What they do:** Website is a physician locator. Providing phone & fax numbers along with other information regarding your physician.
- **Who it serves:** Everyone is welcome to visit the website.
- **Website:** https://www.ocma.org
- **Source:** RCOC community resources spreadsheet (Community Resources 2026)

## Dental and Vision

### Medi-Cal Dental (formerly Denti-Cal)
- **What they do:** Dental coverage for Medi-Cal members.
- **Who it serves:** Medi-Cal and CalOptima members.
- **Phone:** 800-322-6384 (Member Telephone Service Center, Monday–Friday 8:00 AM–5:00 PM)
- **Website:** https://smilecalifornia.org
- **Notes:** Phone and website confirmed on the state Medi-Cal Dental and Smile California sites (2026-10-04). The source list had HICAP's phone and website by mistake.
- **Source:** RCOC community resources spreadsheet (Community Resources 2026)

### Healthy Smiles for Kids of Orange County
- **What they do:** A community-based, non-profit organization serving children in Orange County. The mission is oral health promotion, education and treatment services to reduce the number of cavities among children under age six.
- **Who it serves:** Dental treatment for uninsured children from 0 to 5 years old. Services are provided on a sliding fee scale.
- **Address:** 10602 Chapman Ave. Suite 200, Garden Grove, CA 92840
- **Phone:** 714-537-0700
- **Website:** https://www.healthysmilesoc.org
- **Source:** RCOC community resources spreadsheet (Community Resources 2026)

### VSP Vision Care
- **What they do:** Administers vision services, eye exams and eye glasses.
- **Who it serves:** Anyone needing a low cost vision plan.
- **Phone:** 800-877-7195
- **Website:** https://www.vsp.com
- **Source:** RCOC community resources spreadsheet (Community Resources 2026)

## Clinics

### Lestonnac Free Clinic
- **What they do:** Free medical, dental, and vision services for uninsured and low-income patients, at three Orange County clinics (Orange, Los Alamitos, Fullerton).
- **Who it serves:** Clients who are uninsured and without resources.
- **Address:** Main clinic: 1215 E. Chapman Ave., Orange, CA 92866 · Los Alamitos: 3801 Katella Ave., Suite 212, Los Alamitos, CA 90720 · Fullerton: 505 E. Commonwealth Ave., Fullerton, CA 92831
- **Phone:** Orange (main): 714-633-4600; Los Alamitos: 562-493-4466; Fullerton: 714-399-4571 (fax 714-399-4259)
- **Email:** info@lestonnacfreeclinic.org
- **Website:** https://www.lestonnacfreeclinic.org/contactus
- **Notes:** Main clinic open Monday–Saturday, 9:00 AM–12:00 PM and 1:00–5:00 PM. Los Alamitos clinic has limited hours (including Saturday 9:00 AM–12:00 PM). Some listings show Los Alamitos as Suite 301; the clinic site says Suite 212. The Fullerton clinic shares its address with ICNA Relief. Also clinics in Los Angeles County and the Inland Empire. Patients pay for medication and lab orders. All three Orange County phones confirmed on the clinic website (2026-10-05).
- **Source:** RCOC community resources spreadsheet (Community Resources 2026)

### Planned Parenthood – Orange
- **What they do:** Planned Parenthood offers health education, sex and relationship, reproductive health, and appropriate personal behavior. Also, provides reproductive healthcare, birth control, prenatal care, STD testing, and treatment for males and females. Bilingual staff.
- **Who it serves:** Accepts Medi-Cal or you may qualify for a state-funded program or a lower fee scale. If you worried about cost, talk to them about how you can get health care that fits your budget
- **Phone:** 714-922-4100
- **Website:** https://www.plannedparenthood.org
- **Source:** RCOC community resources spreadsheet (Community Resources 2026)

## Specialty Care

### Shriners Children's Southern California
- **What they do:** Care for orthopedic conditions, prosthetics and orthotics, hand disorders, burn scars and cleft lip and palate.
- **Who it serves:** Care is provided for children under age 18 regardless of family’s ability to pay.
- **Address:** 909 S. Fair Oaks Ave., Pasadena, CA 91105
- **Phone:** 626-389-9300; 888-486-KIDS (5437)
- **Website:** https://www.shrinerschildrens.org
- **Notes:** Located in Pasadena, outside Orange County. Phone confirmed on the Shriners Children's website (2026-10-05).
- **Source:** RCOC community resources spreadsheet (Community Resources 2026)
