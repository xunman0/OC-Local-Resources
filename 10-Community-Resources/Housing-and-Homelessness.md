# Housing and Homelessness

Rental assistance, housing authorities, fair housing, homelessness help, and care facility licensing.

**Status:** copied from source lists dated 2026. Not yet checked with each organization.

> [!important] Call before you go
> Programs, phone numbers, and eligibility rules change. Call the organization or check its website before visiting. For help finding any service in Orange County, dial **2-1-1** (24/7, multilingual).

## In This File

- Homelessness: Family Solutions Collaborative Access Point Network

## Homelessness

### Family Solutions Collaborative Access Point Network
- **What they do:** The Family Solutions Collaborative is a coalition of the leading family service nonprofits in Orange County, and was formed to streamline the process for homeless families to be connected to the services that they need.
- **Who it serves:** Homeless and at-risk families
- **Phone:** 2-1-1 or 888-600-4357
- **Website:** https://www.211oc.org
- **Notes:** Homeless families connect through 211 OC.
- **Source:** RCOC community resources spreadsheet (Community Resources 2026)

## Housing Authorities and Rental Assistance

### Anaheim Housing Authority
- **What they do:** Rental subsidies for very low income families (Section 8) rentals. No emergency housing. Provides counseling, education and outreach to buyers, owners and renters.
- **Who it serves:** For low income, the elderly or people with disabilities. Preference is given to those who live or work in Anaheim.
- **Address:** 201 S. Anaheim Blvd., Suite 201, Anaheim, CA 92805
- **Phone:** 714-765-4320; information line 714-765-4300 ext. 4886
- **Email:** AHAinfo@anaheim.net
- **Website:** https://www.anaheim.net
- **Notes:** Phone confirmed on the City of Anaheim website (2026-10-04). Walk-in hours have changed; call before visiting.
- **Source:** RCOC community resources spreadsheet (Community Resources 2026)

### Garden Grove Housing Authority
- **What they do:** Provides rental assistance and housing vouchers.
- **Who it serves:** For low income, the elderly or people with disabilities
- **Address:** 12966 Euclid St., Suite 150, Garden Grove, CA 92840
- **Phone:** 714-741-5150
- **Website:** https://www.ggcity.org/housing-authority
- **Source:** RCOC community resources spreadsheet (Community Resources 2026)

### Orange County Housing Authority
- **What they do:** Provides rental assistance and housing vouchers. Serves all of Orange Co. EXCEPT Anaheim, Garden Grove and Santa Ana. (Refer to the individual entries for these other cities.)
- **Who it serves:** For low income, elderly or people with disabilities.
- **Phone:** 714-480-2700
- **Website:** https://www.ochousing.org
- **Source:** RCOC community resources spreadsheet (Community Resources 2026)

### Santa Ana Housing Authority
- **What they do:** Provides rental assistance and housing vouchers.
- **Who it serves:** For low income Orange County residents with primary focus on those living or working in Santa Ana.
- **Address:** 20 Civic Center Plaza, Santa Ana, CA 92701
- **Phone:** 714-667-2284 (fax 714-547-5411)
- **Website:** https://www.santa-ana.org/departments/housing-authority/
- **Notes:** Office hours Monday–Thursday, 8:00 AM–5:00 PM. Phone and ZIP updated from the City of Santa Ana website (2026-10-05); the source had 714-667-2200 and ZIP 92702.
- **Source:** RCOC community resources spreadsheet (Community Resources 2026)

## Care Facility Licensing

### California Department of Public Health – Licensing and Certification
- **What they do:** Regulates health care facilities to ensure their compliance with state licensing and federal certification regulations.
- **Who it serves:** All health care facilities and programs or the resident of a health care licensed facility.
- **Phone:** 800-228-5234
- **Website:** https://www.cdph.ca.gov
- **Source:** RCOC community resources spreadsheet (Community Resources 2026)

### Community Care Licensing (CA Department of Social Services)
- **What they do:** Licenses and monitors community care facilities: child care, board and care homes, group homes, small family homes, adult residential facilities, and residential facilities for the elderly.
- **Who it serves:** Any community care facility or resident of a community care facility.
- **Address:** 750 The City Drive, Suite 250, Orange, CA 92868
- **Phone:** 714-703-2800; 714-703-2840
- **Website:** https://www.cdss.ca.gov/inforesources/community-care-licensing
- **Notes:** Listed twice in the source (under Child Care and Housing); combined here. The source labels the two numbers "Child Care" and "Adult Care" but the order is unclear.
- **Source:** RCOC community resources spreadsheet (Community Resources 2026)

## Housing Information and Rights

### Fair Housing Council of OC
- **What they do:** Information and support regarding fair housing laws and regulations. The Council can assist with fair housing enforcement, HUD counseling, landlord-tenant issues and mediation.
- **Who it serves:** Anyone can call for assistance.
- **Address:** 1516 Brookhollow Drive Suite A, Santa Ana, CA 92705
- **Phone:** 714-569-0823; 800-698-FAIR (3247)
- **Website:** https://www.fairhousingoc.org
- **Source:** RCOC community resources spreadsheet (Community Resources 2026)

### U.S. Department of Housing and Urban Development (HUD) – Santa Ana Office
- **What they do:** Information regarding renting, buying, home accessibility, Fair Housing rights and many other topics. Website offers easy to use locator to find subsidized housing in your area.
- **Who it serves:** Everyone is welcome
- **Address:** 34 Civic Center Plaza Room 7015, Santa Ana, CA 92701-4003
- **Phone:** 714-796-5577 (TTY 714-796-5517)
- **Website:** https://www.hud.gov
- **Source:** RCOC community resources spreadsheet (Community Resources 2026)

### Innovative Housing Opportunities
- **What they do:** Contracted with RCOC to provide housing consultation and support to families, person served and service providers regarding Section 8 HUD, fair housing and resources.
- **Who it serves:** RCOC person served families and service providers.
- **Phone:** 949-863-9740
- **Website:** https://www.innovativehousing.com
- **Source:** RCOC community resources spreadsheet (Community Resources 2026)
