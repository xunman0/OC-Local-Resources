# Mental Health, Crisis, and Protective Services

Mental health services, crisis lines, and abuse reporting hotlines.

**Status:** copied from source lists dated 2026. Not yet checked with each organization.

> [!important] Call before you go
> Programs, phone numbers, and eligibility rules change. Call the organization or check its website before visiting. For help finding any service in Orange County, dial **2-1-1** (24/7, multilingual).

## In This File

- Mental Health Services: Child Guidance Center, Children & Youth Behavioral Health (County of Orange), NAMI Orange County (National Alliance on Mental Illness), USC Telebehavioral Health Online Clinic, Western Youth Services
- Crisis Lines: Crisis & Suicide Prevention Hotline
- Abuse Reporting: Adult Protective Services (APS), Child Protective Services (CPS)

## Mental Health Services

### Child Guidance Center
- **What they do:** Child Guidance Center provides evidence-based mental health services, from our flagship Parent Child Interaction Therapy program to intensive approaches for addressing the impact of trauma on children and families.
- **Who it serves:** Works with families having Medi-Cal only.
- **Phone:** 714-953-4455
- **Website:** https://www.childguidancecenteroc.org
- **Notes:** Listed under "Immigration" in the source, but it is a children's mental health agency.
- **Source:** RCOC community resources spreadsheet (Community Resources 2026)

### Children & Youth Behavioral Health (County of Orange)
- **What they do:** Children and Youth Behavioral Health (CYBH) provides a broad range of services for behaviorally, emotionally or mentally disordered children and adolescents.
- **Who it serves:** Children and Adolescents
- **Phone:** 855-625-4657 (OC Links)
- **Website:** https://www.ochealthinfo.com
- **Source:** RCOC community resources spreadsheet (Community Resources 2026)

### NAMI Orange County (National Alliance on Mental Illness)
- **What they do:** Provides direct services, advocacy, information, hospital support groups, support groups, peer connector, and more services please visit their website to learn more about the array of services.
- **Who it serves:** Families and individuals living with mental illness.
- **Address:** 1810 E. 17th Street, Santa Ana, CA 92705
- **Phone:** 714-544-8488
- **Website:** https://www.namioc.org
- **Source:** RCOC community resources spreadsheet (Community Resources 2026)

### USC Telebehavioral Health Online Clinic
- **What they do:** Free online counseling and therapy by video, in English and Spanish, through the USC Suzanne Dworak-Peck School of Social Work.
- **Who it serves:** Individuals and families
- **Phone:** 866-740-6502
- **Email:** usc.telehealth@usc.edu
- **Website:** https://dworakpeck.usc.edu/TBHC
- **Notes:** Call to register. Other listings say it serves ages 12 and up. Phone and website confirmed (2026-10-05).
- **Source:** RCOC community resources spreadsheet (Community Resources 2026)

### Western Youth Services
- **What they do:** Western Youth Services provides outpatient mental health services primarily for Orange County. Services including but not limited to clinical interventions, clinical and psycho-diagnostic assessment, family therapy, and many other services.
- **Who it serves:** Medi-Cal eligible clients up to age twenty-one years old.
- **Phone:** 949-855-1556
- **Website:** https://www.westernyouthservices.org
- **Source:** RCOC community resources spreadsheet (Community Resources 2026)

## Crisis Lines

### Crisis & Suicide Prevention Hotline
- **What they do:** Support for those struggling with suicide.
- **Who it serves:** Anyone contemplating suicide.
- **Address:** 2021 E. 4th Street, #204, Santa Ana, CA 92705
- **Phone:** 877-727-4747
- **Notes:** **In a crisis, you can also call or text 988** (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline), available 24/7.
- **Source:** RCOC community resources spreadsheet (Community Resources 2026)

## Abuse Reporting

### Adult Protective Services (APS)
- **What they do:** Prevents and investigates neglect, abuse, or exploitation of adults who cannot protect their own interests.
- **Who it serves:** 65 or older, or physically, mentally, or developmentally disabled 18-64 adults.
- **Phone:** 800-451-5155 (hotline)
- **Website:** https://ssa.ocgov.com/abuse/elder
- **Source:** RCOC community resources spreadsheet (Community Resources 2026)

### Child Protective Services (CPS)
- **What they do:** Investigation of suspected abuse of children under age 18.
- **Who it serves:** Anyone
- **Phone:** 800-207-4464 (hotline)
- **Website:** https://www.cdss.ca.gov
- **Source:** RCOC community resources spreadsheet (Community Resources 2026)
