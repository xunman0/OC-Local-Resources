# Legal Aid and Advocacy

Free or low-cost legal help, disability rights advocacy, and complaint and oversight agencies.

**Status:** copied from source lists dated 2026. Not yet checked with each organization.

> Programs, phone numbers, and eligibility rules change. Call the organization or check its website before visiting. For help finding any service in Orange County, dial **2-1-1** (24/7, multilingual).

## Legal Help

### Community Legal Aid SoCal (formerly Legal Aid Society of Orange County)
- **What they do:** Provides free general legal services including family law, landlord tenant disputes, bankruptcy, dispute mediation, small claims court and referrals.
- **Who it serves:** Low income Orange County residents.
- **Address:** 2101 N. Tustin Ave., Santa Ana, CA 92705
- **Phone:** 714-571-5200; 800-834-5001
- **Website:** https://www.communitylegalsocal.org
- **Source:** RCOC community resources spreadsheet (Community Resources 2026); Legal Resources list (Orange County and surrounding area)

### Public Law Center
- **What they do:** Through volunteers and staff, free civil legal services are provided, including counseling, individual representation, community education, and strategic litigation and advocacy to challenge societal injustices.
- **Who it serves:** They serve a wide range of clients including immigrants, minorities, veterans, seniors, children and low-income residents of Orange County.
- **Address:** 601 Civic Center Dr. West, Santa Ana, CA 92701
- **Phone:** 714-541-1010
- **Website:** https://www.publiclawcenter.org
- **Source:** RCOC community resources spreadsheet (Community Resources 2026); Legal Resources list (Orange County and surrounding area)

### Child Care Law Center
- **What they do:** Legal services and publications related to child care laws including the Americans with Disabilities Act (ADA).
- **Who it serves:** Anyone
- **Address:** 445 Church St., San Francisco, CA 94114
- **Phone:** 415-558-8005
- **Website:** https://www.childcarelaw.org
- **Notes:** Statewide organization based in San Francisco, outside Orange County.
- **Source:** RCOC community resources spreadsheet (Community Resources 2026)

## Disability Rights

### Disability Rights California (Clients' Rights Advocate)
- **What they do:** Protects the rights of individuals with developmental or mental disabilities. Advocacy Services.
- **Who it serves:** Individuals with mental or developmental disabilities.
- **Address:** 17291 Irvine Blvd. Suite 465, Tustin, CA 92780
- **Phone:** 714-621-0563 (fax 714-621-0550)
- **Website:** https://www.disabilityrightsca.org
- **Notes:** Website was cut off in the source list ("disabilityrightsca.or"); corrected to .org.
- **Source:** RCOC community resources spreadsheet (Community Resources 2026)

## Oversight and Complaints

### Long-Term Care Ombudsman
- **What they do:** Volunteer program providing assistance in resolving conflicts for members in a long-term care facility.
- **Who it serves:** Persons age 65 or older residing in a long-term care facility.
- **Address:** 2 Executive Circle, Suite 175, Irvine, CA 92614
- **Phone:** 714-479-0107
- **Website:** https://www.coasc.org
- **Notes:** Run by the Council on Aging – Southern California.
- **Source:** RCOC community resources spreadsheet (Community Resources 2026)
