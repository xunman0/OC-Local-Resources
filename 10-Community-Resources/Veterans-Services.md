# Veterans Services

Benefits help, housing help, and crisis support for veterans and their families.

**Status:** copied from source lists dated 2026. Not yet checked with each organization.

> [!important] Call before you go
> Programs, phone numbers, and eligibility rules change. Call the organization or check its website before visiting. For help finding any service in Orange County, dial **2-1-1** (24/7, multilingual).

## In This File

- Benefits and Services: OC4Vets, OC Veterans Service Office
- Housing Help: VA Community Resource and Referral Center (CRRC), Volunteers of America – Veterans Services, 1736 Family Crisis Center – Veterans Services
- Crisis Support: Veterans Crisis Line

## Benefits and Services

### OC4Vets
- **What they do:** Helps veterans and their families find community and behavioral health services.
- **Phone:** 855-OC-LINKS (855-625-4657)
- **Notes:** 24/7.
- **Source:** County of Orange Pocket Guide Resource Directory (Central, North, South SPAs; updated 5/13/2025)

### OC Veterans Service Office
- **What they do:** Helps veterans, dependents, and survivors get VA benefits.
- **Address:** 1300 S. Grand Ave., Building B, 2nd Floor, Santa Ana, CA 92705
- **Phone:** 714-480-6555
- **Notes:** Monday–Friday, 8:00 AM–4:00 PM.
- **Source:** County of Orange Pocket Guide Resource Directory (Central, North, South SPAs; updated 5/13/2025)

## Housing Help

### VA Community Resource and Referral Center (CRRC)
- **What they do:** Helps veterans experiencing homelessness with housing placement and VA health care and benefits.
- **Address:** 888 W. Santa Ana Blvd., Suite 150, Santa Ana, CA 92701
- **Phone:** 844-838-8300
- **Notes:** Monday–Friday, 8:00 AM–4:00 PM.
- **Source:** County of Orange Pocket Guide Resource Directory (Central, North, South SPAs; updated 5/13/2025)

### Volunteers of America – Veterans Services
- **What they do:** Rental assistance, case management, and financial planning for veterans who are homeless or at risk; peer support for veterans and active-duty military.
- **Address:** 2100 N. Broadway, Suite 300, Santa Ana, CA 92705
- **Phone:** 714-426-9834
- **Notes:** Monday–Friday, 8:00 AM–4:30 PM.
- **Source:** County of Orange Pocket Guide Resource Directory (Central, North, South SPAs; updated 5/13/2025)

### 1736 Family Crisis Center – Veterans Services
- **What they do:** Rental assistance, case management, and financial planning for veterans who are homeless or at risk.
- **Address:** 888 W. Santa Ana Blvd., Suite 200, Santa Ana, CA 92701
- **Phone:** 424-772-5668 (Central and North guides); 866-249-2557 (South guide)
- **Notes:** Monday–Friday, 8:00 AM–4:00 PM. The pocket guides list two different phone numbers; call either.
- **Source:** County of Orange Pocket Guide Resource Directory (Central, North, South SPAs; updated 5/13/2025)

## Crisis Support

### Veterans Crisis Line
- **Phone:** Dial 988, then press 1 (pocket guide lists 800-273-8255, press 1, which connects to the same line)
- **Website:** https://www.veteranscrisisline.net
- **Notes:** 24/7, for veterans and their families.
- **Source:** County of Orange Pocket Guide Resource Directory (Central, North, South SPAs; updated 5/13/2025)
