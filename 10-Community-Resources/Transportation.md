# Transportation

Paratransit, driver training for people with disabilities, and airport assistance.

**Status:** copied from source lists dated 2026. Not yet checked with each organization.

> [!important] Call before you go
> Programs, phone numbers, and eligibility rules change. Call the organization or check its website before visiting. For help finding any service in Orange County, dial **2-1-1** (24/7, multilingual).

## In This File

- Driver Training: Handicap Driver Training, Driver Rehabilitation & Training Program – Rancho Los Amigos, MasterDrive of Orange County
- Airport Assistance: Helping Hands – John Wayne Airport
- Public Transit and Paratransit: OCTA (Orange County Transportation Authority) and OC ACCESS Paratransit

## Driver Training

### Handicap Driver Training
- **What they do:** Handicap Driver Training is devoted primarily to assist those in need of training with special needs.
- **Who it serves:** Persons with developmental disabilities.
- **Phone:** 951-515-5125
- **Website:** https://www.handicapdrivertraining.com
- **Notes:** Serves all of Southern California. The source lists an instructor's name; left out here.
- **Source:** RCOC community resources spreadsheet (Community Resources 2026)

### Driver Rehabilitation & Training Program – Rancho Los Amigos
- **What they do:** A hospital- based program requiring a physician’s referral, medical history records, and health insurance information. A valid driver license or instruction permit is needed for any on-road evaluation and training.
- **Who it serves:** People with age-related changes, and other disabilities.
- **Phone:** 562-385-6847
- **Website:** https://www.rancho.org
- **Notes:** Hospital-based (Downey, outside Orange County). Requires a physician's referral, medical history, and insurance information. A valid license or permit is needed for on-road training.
- **Source:** RCOC community resources spreadsheet (Community Resources 2026)

### MasterDrive of Orange County
- **What they do:** Unique driver training program for individuals with ADHD, ASD, CP and other challenges.
- **Who it serves:** Call for additional information.
- **Address:** 16 Technology Drive, Suite 160, Irvine, CA 92618
- **Phone:** 949-341-0031
- **Website:** https://www.masterdrive.com
- **Notes:** Source listed Suite 130; current listings show Suite 160 (checked 2026-10-04).
- **Source:** RCOC community resources spreadsheet (Community Resources 2026)

## Airport Assistance

### Helping Hands – John Wayne Airport
- **What they do:** Free help at John Wayne Airport for travelers with special needs, daily 6:00 AM–11:00 PM. Call or email before arriving.
- **Who it serves:** Individuals with disabilities, such as Autism
- **Address:** 18601 Airport Way, Santa Ana, CA 92707
- **Phone:** 949-252-5200
- **Email:** info@ocair.com
- **Website:** https://www.ocair.com
- **Source:** RCOC community resources spreadsheet (Community Resources 2026)

## Public Transit and Paratransit

### OCTA (Orange County Transportation Authority) and OC ACCESS Paratransit
- **What they do:** Primary transportation planning agency and transit provider. Provides curb-to-curb transportation for persons with a disability who cannot take regular fixed route buses.
- **Who it serves:** Any person may ride the fixed route bus. ACCESS paratransit is available to eligible persons with disabilities at any age.
- **Address:** 550 S. Main Street, Orange, CA 92868
- **Phone:** Bus information: 714-636-7433; ACCESS: 877-628-2232
- **Website:** https://www.octa.net
- **Source:** RCOC community resources spreadsheet (Community Resources 2026)
