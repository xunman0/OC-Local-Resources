# Homelessness Prevention and Housing Help for Families

For Orange County families who are behind on rent, have received an eviction notice, or are about to lose their housing. Organized by what to do first.

**Status:** Entries marked ✅ were checked on the organization's website or official listings (2026-10-05) or come from earlier verified entries in this knowledge base.

> [!important] Call before you go
> Shelter beds and rent help fill up fast, and rules change. Call before going anywhere. For help finding any service in Orange County, dial **2-1-1** (24/7, multilingual).

> [!warning] Got court eviction papers? Act the same day.
> An eviction notice from a landlord is not the same as being evicted. But if you are served court papers (a Summons and a Complaint for Unlawful Detainer), you have **10 court days** to file a response (form UD-105, "Answer"). Weekends and court holidays don't count. If you miss the deadline, the landlord can win without a trial. Contact free legal help right away (see Step 2), or go to the court's Self-Help Center. If you can't afford the filing fee ($240–$450), ask for a fee waiver. *Source: California Courts Self-Help Guide (checked 2026-10-05). This is general information, not legal advice.*

## Step 2 – Legal Help to Stop or Delay an Eviction

### Stay Housed OC ✅
- **What they do:** Free online tool to help Orange County renters (and landlords) understand their rights and prevent eviction. Connects tenants to:
  - **Free legal help** from lawyers for eligible tenants going through the eviction process
  - **Free mediation** with the landlord, including payment plans and move-out dates
  - **Workshops and clinics**, in person and online
  - **Guides, videos, and court forms** for people filing paperwork themselves
  - Partner services such as rental assistance, financial coaching, and job training
- **Who runs it:** The OC Eviction Diversion Collaborative, led by Orange County United Way.
- **Website:** https://stayhousedoc.org
- **Note:** As of 2026-10-05, the Stay Housed OC "Eviction Process" page still says tenants have 5 business days to respond. California law changed this to **10 court days** in 2025 (confirmed on the California Courts Self-Help Guide).
- **Key pages:** The Eviction Process (https://stayhousedoc.org/the-eviction-process/), Current Laws (https://stayhousedoc.org/current-laws/), Legal Help (https://stayhousedoc.org/legal-help/), Mediation (https://stayhousedoc.org/mediation-support/)
- **Source:** Stay Housed OC website (checked 2026-10-05)

### CalWORKs Homeless Assistance ✅
- **What they do:** For families with children who receive or qualify for CalWORKs:
  - **Temporary Homeless Assistance:** payments for temporary shelter (such as a motel) for up to **16 days** in a 12-month period, with some exceptions.
  - **Permanent Homeless Assistance:** help with a **security deposit and last month's rent** to move into housing, or up to **two months of back rent** to prevent eviction.
- **How to apply:** County of Orange Social Services Agency, or online at BenefitsCal (https://benefitscal.com).
- **Website:** https://www.cdss.ca.gov
- **Source:** California Department of Social Services (checked 2026-10-05)

### Serving People in Need (SPIN) ✅
- **What they do:** Help with **move-in costs** for families with minor children, plus case management and support services.
- **Phone:** 714-751-1101 ext. 14 or 17
- **Hours:** Monday–Friday, 8:30 AM–5:00 PM
- **Website:** https://spinoc.org/contact
- **Source:** County of Orange Pocket Guide (5/2025)

### Housing Authorities
Rental assistance programs, including Housing Choice Vouchers (Section 8). Waitlists are usually long, so these rarely help in an emergency. See [[Housing-and-Homelessness]] for Orange County, Anaheim, Santa Ana, and Garden Grove.

## Step 5 – Keep Children in School

### McKinney-Vento School Rights ✅
- **What it is:** A federal law that protects children without stable housing, including families staying with relatives, in motels, in shelters, or in cars.
- **Children have the right to:**
  - Stay in their current school, even after moving
  - Get transportation to that school
  - Enroll right away, even without the usual documents
- **Who to contact:** Every school district has a homeless education liaison. Ask the school office for the McKinney-Vento liaison.
- **Countywide help:** Orange County Department of Education, McKinney-Vento Educational Services: **714-966-3538**. OCDE's HOPES Collaborative page lists every district's liaison (https://www.ocde.us).
- **Source:** Orange County Department of Education website (checked 2026-10-05)
