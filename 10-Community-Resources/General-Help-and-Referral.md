# General Help and Referral

Hotlines, basic-needs agencies, and food organizations that connect people to many kinds of help.

**Status:** copied from source lists dated 2026. Not yet checked with each organization.

> [!important] Call before you go
> Programs, phone numbers, and eligibility rules change. Call the organization or check its website before visiting. For help finding any service in Orange County, dial **2-1-1** (24/7, multilingual).

## In This File

- Food: Second Harvest Food Bank of Orange County, Share Our Selves (SOS), Community Action Partnership of Orange County (CAP OC) – OC Food Bank
- Community Agencies: Korean Community Services, Salvation Army – Orange County Family Services, Southland Integrated Services, Inc.
- Hotlines: Orange County 2-1-1

## Food

### Second Harvest Food Bank of Orange County
- **What they do:** Provides food and groceries for qualifying persons.
- **Who it serves:** Low income elderly, disabled and the working poor.
- **Address:** 8014 Marine Way, Irvine, CA 92618
- **Phone:** 949-653-2900
- **Website:** https://www.feedoc.org
- **Notes:** Supplies most of the food pantries in this knowledge base. Call for the latest partner pantry schedules. See [[_Index|Food Pantries by City]].
- **Source:** RCOC community resources spreadsheet (Community Resources 2026)

### Share Our Selves (SOS)
- **What they do:** Emergency food and emergency financial aid available. Provides a walk-in medical clinic. Dental clinic appointments available once per month.
- **Who it serves:** Resident of Orange County.
- **Address:** 1550 Superior Ave., Costa Mesa, CA 92627
- **Phone:** 949-270-2100
- **Website:** https://www.shareourselves.org
- **Notes:** Food pantry hours: Monday–Friday 8:00 AM–4:30 PM, registration on arrival (confirmed 2026-10-03). See [[Costa-Mesa]].
- **Source:** RCOC community resources spreadsheet (Community Resources 2026)

### Community Action Partnership of Orange County (CAP OC) – OC Food Bank
- **What they do:** Food bank serving low-income residents and people with disabilities. Also helps families enroll in emergency utility assistance. Runs the CAP OC Diaper Bank.
- **Who it serves:** Low income.
- **Address:** 11870 Monarch St., Garden Grove, CA 92841
- **Phone:** 714-897-6670
- **Email:** info@capoc.org
- **Website:** https://www.capoc.org
- **Notes:** Source listed ocfoodbank.org. See [[Diaper-Resources]].
- **Source:** RCOC community resources spreadsheet (Community Resources 2026)

## Community Agencies

### Korean Community Services
- **What they do:** Diverse programs aimed at improving life for Koreans within the community. Referral and navigation assistance for families with children who have DD.
- **Who it serves:** All Korean families
- **Address:** 7212 Orangethorpe Ave., Suite 9A, Buena Park, CA 90621
- **Phone:** 714-503-6550
- **Website:** https://www.koreancommunity.org
- **Notes:** Also hosts a Second Harvest Park-it Market (Thursdays). See [[Buena-Park]].
- **Source:** RCOC community resources spreadsheet (Community Resources 2026)

### Salvation Army – Orange County Family Services
- **What they do:** Partial rent and utility assistance, plus help with other basic needs such as clothing and household items.
- **Who it serves:** Required documentation includes picture I.D., proof of address and amount of rent, birth certificates, proof of income.
- **Phone:** Family Services: 714-783-2344; Santa Ana Temple: 714-545-2937
- **Email:** ocfamilyservices@usw.salvationarmy.org
- **Website:** https://orangecounty.salvationarmy.org
- **Notes:** Bring photo ID, proof of address and rent amount, birth certificates, and proof of income. Family Services phone and email from the Salvation Army Orange County website (checked 2026-10-05). The Anaheim office (1515 W. North St.) also runs a food pantry; see [[Anaheim]].
- **Source:** RCOC community resources spreadsheet (Community Resources 2026)

### Southland Integrated Services, Inc.
- **What they do:** Provides the following programs: CHANGES in which they dispute resolution services involving gas and electricity bills; Citizenship & Immigration Services; Early Intervention Services for Older Adults; Food distribution; transportation for seniors with non emergency medical appointments; telecommunication education, and yoga classes.
- **Who it serves:** Low income residents, the elderly, children, and people with mental disabilities
- **Address:** 1618 W. First St., Santa Ana, CA 92703
- **Phone:** 714-620-7000
- **Website:** https://www.southlandintegrated.org
- **Notes:** Not related to Southlands Church or Church of Southland.
- **Source:** RCOC community resources spreadsheet (Community Resources 2026)

## Hotlines

### Orange County 2-1-1
- **What they do:** Health and Human Services Hotline for help in finding services needed, i.e. where to go for a hot meal, where to get free low-cost health care, information on child care, care for aging parents, and a variety of other resources in Orange County and Southern California.
- **Who it serves:** Available 24 hours per day, 7 days per week. It is confidential and multilingual assistance is available.
- **Phone:** 2-1-1 or 888-600-4357
- **Website:** https://www.211oc.org
- **Notes:** Available 24/7, confidential, with multilingual help.
- **Source:** RCOC community resources spreadsheet (Community Resources 2026); Legal Resources list (Orange County and surrounding area)
