Early childhood, child care, special education, and school information.

> Programs, phone numbers, and eligibility rules change. Call the organization or check its website before visiting. For help finding any service in Orange County, dial **2-1-1** (24/7, multilingual).

## Special Education

### Team of Advocates for Special Kids (TASK)
- **What they do:** Helps families understand their rights, request assessments, prepare for IEP meetings, and plan transitions. Also helps families explore assistive technology for learning, communication, and independence.
- **Who it serves:** People with disabilities from birth to age 26 and their families. For assistive technology, all ages.
- **Address:** 3040 Saturn St., #103, Brea, CA 92821
- **Phone:** 714-533-8275
- **Website:** https://www.taskca.org
- **Notes:** Listed twice in the source (Individual and Family Training, and Education); combined here.
- **Source:** RCOC community resources spreadsheet (Community Resources 2026)

### Orange County Department of Education (OCDE)
- **What they do:** Operates programs for children who are eligible for special education. Provides outreach, training and coordination of education programs.
- **Who it serves:** Please contact your local school district to initiate the referral process.
- **Address:** 200 Kalmus Dr., Costa Mesa, CA 92626
- **Phone:** 714-966-4000
- **Website:** https://www.ocde.us
- **Source:** RCOC community resources spreadsheet (Community Resources 2026)

### Wrightslaw (website)
- **What they do:** Accurate, reliable information about special education law, education law, and advocacy for children with disabilities. There are thousands of articles, cases, and free resources on various topics.
- **Who it serves:** Anyone.
- **Website:** https://www.wrightslaw.com
- **Source:** RCOC community resources spreadsheet (Community Resources 2026)

## Child Care

### Children’s Home Society of California
- **What they do:** Assistance for families in locating licensed child care programs. Online portal provides free referrals to licensed childcare programs.
- **Who it serves:** Financial assistance can be available for low income families living in Orange County.
- **Address:** 333 South Anita Drive, Suite 350, Orange, CA 92868
- **Phone:** 714-543-2273; 949-364-6605
- **Website:** https://www.chs-ca.org
- **Notes:** Source link pointed to the child care referrals page on this site.
- **Source:** RCOC community resources spreadsheet (Community Resources 2026)

## School Information

### Niche – Find Your School (website)
- **What they do:** Look up California schools: address, phone, district contacts, and attendance boundary maps where available.

- **Source:** RCOC community resources spreadsheet (Community Resources 2026)

## Early Childhood

### Orange County Head Start, Inc.
- **What they do:** Provides educational programs for young children, 0-4, at locations throughout Orange County.
- **Who it serves:** Eligibility determined by multiple factors including income and special needs of child
- **Address:** 2501 S. Pullman St., #100, Santa Ana, CA 92705
- **Phone:** 714-241-8920
- **Website:** https://www.ochsinc.org
- **Source:** RCOC community resources spreadsheet (Community Resources 2026)

### Help Me Grow Orange County
- **What they do:** Connects families and children with developmental, behavioral and/or learning concerns to existing resources in the community.
- **Who it serves:** For all children, birth through five living in Orange County.
- **Address:** 1120 W. La Veta Ave. Suite 450, Orange, CA 92868
- **Phone:** 866-476-9025
- **Website:** https://www.helpmegrowoc.org
- **Source:** RCOC community resources spreadsheet (Community Resources 2026)
