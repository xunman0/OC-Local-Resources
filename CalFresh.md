# CalFresh

**CalFresh** is California's name for SNAP (formerly called food stamps). It gives eligible households money every month on an **EBT card** to buy groceries.

**Status:** General program information. Income limits and benefit amounts change every year (usually in October). Check BenefitsCal or call the county for current rules. This is not legal advice.

> [!tip] Need food fast?
> If your household has very little income and money, ask for **expedited (emergency) CalFresh**. You may get benefits **within 3 days** of applying. While you wait, see [[01-How-to-Get-Food-Now]].

## What CalFresh Pays For
- **Can buy:** most groceries, including fruits, vegetables, meat, dairy, bread, and seeds or plants that grow food. Accepted at most grocery stores and many farmers' markets.
- **Cannot buy:** alcohol, tobacco, vitamins, pet food, hot prepared foods (in most cases), and non-food items like diapers, soap, and paper products. For diapers, see [[Diaper-Resources]].

## Who May Qualify
Eligibility depends on household size, income, and some expenses like rent and child care. Many people qualify who don't expect to, including:
- Working families and people with low-paying or part-time jobs
- Seniors and people with disabilities
- **People who receive SSI** (California allows SSI recipients to get CalFresh)
- Some college students (special rules apply)
- **Mixed-status families:** children or other household members who are eligible can receive benefits even if others in the household are not.

**Immigrants:** Some immigrants qualify; rules depend on status. If you are worried about how benefits might affect an immigration case, talk to an immigration legal service first. See [[Immigration-Resources]].

## What Happens After You Apply
1. **Interview:** A county worker will call or meet with you. Answer the phone, and call back if you miss it.
2. **Documents:** You may be asked for ID, proof of income, rent, and other expenses.
3. **Decision:** Usually within 30 days, or about 3 days for expedited service.
4. **EBT card:** Benefits are loaded each month.
5. **Keep it active:** Report changes and complete renewals on time, or benefits can stop.
