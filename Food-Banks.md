# Major Food Banks in Orange County

Orange County has two large food banks. **Food banks are warehouses.** They supply food to hundreds of local pantries, churches, schools, and community centers. **Most people get food at a partner site, not at the food bank itself.** To find a partner site near you, see [[_Index|Food Pantries by City]] or [[01-How-to-Get-Food-Now]].

## Second Harvest Food Bank of Orange County
- **What they do:** Supplies most of the food pantries in this knowledge base: about 290 partner sites in 33 cities.
- **Phone:** 949-653-2900
- **Email:** info@feedoc.org
- **Address:** 8014 Marine Way, Irvine, CA 92618
- **Website:** https://www.feedoc.org
- **Contact them for:** the **latest schedule at any partner site**, especially school sites (please contact Second Harvest instead of the school).

### Types of Second Harvest partner sites
The partner list marks sites with short codes. In this knowledge base:
| Code | Meaning | Who it serves |
|---|---|---|
| (none) | Community food pantry (church, nonprofit, community center) | Usually open to the public; some have city, ZIP, or ID rules |
| School name | Food distribution at a school | May be limited to that school's families; call Second Harvest |
| **SG** | Senior Grocery, sometimes with TEFAP | Older adults; age and income rules may apply |
| **PIM** | Park-it Market (mobile market) | Some marked "open site"; others may be for residents only. See [[Hot-Meals-and-Mobile]] |
| **KC** | Kids meal site | Children 18 and under, usually enrolled in that program |
| **PSP** | Appears on some school market sites | Meaning not stated in the source list |

**TEFAP** (The Emergency Food Assistance Program) is a federal program that supplies USDA food through food banks, with its own income rules.

## Community Action Partnership of Orange County (CAP OC) – OC Food Bank
- **What they do:** Food bank serving low-income residents and people with disabilities. Also runs the **CAP OC Diaper Bank** and helps families enroll in emergency utility assistance.
- **Phone:** 714-897-6670
- **Email:** info@capoc.org
- **Address:** 11870 Monarch Street, Garden Grove, CA 92841
- **Website:** https://www.capoc.org
- **Contact them for:** the latest **diaper partner list**. See [[Diaper-Resources]].
- **Also runs:** Southwest Community Center in Santa Ana (hot meals, food distribution, diapers). See [[Santa-Ana]].
