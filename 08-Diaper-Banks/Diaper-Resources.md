# Free Diaper Resources in Orange County

**Last verified:** 2026-10-03 (5 sites also checked on their own websites). Diaper supplies run out and schedules change, so always call before going.

> [!important] Call before you go
> Most diaper partners serve only registered clients or work by appointment, and supplies are limited. Call the site first to ask about eligibility, sizes, and what to bring. If you can't reach them, dial 2-1-1.

## Quick Summary
Most free diapers in Orange County come through the **Community Action Partnership of Orange County (CAP OC) Diaper Bank**, which gives monthly diaper supplies to families with children through partner agencies. This file lists 29 CAP OC partners in 13 cities, plus details from the food pantry lists.

- **Most partners require you to call first, make an appointment, or already be a registered client.** Very few are walk-in.
- **WIC offices:** 10 WIC offices are CAP OC partners. Most say you must be a registered WIC client and call first.
- **Evening and weekend options:** Delhi Center (Santa Ana) is open evenings Monday–Thursday and on Saturdays. Share Our Selves (Costa Mesa) is open until 7 PM Monday–Thursday.
- **Cities with partners:** Anaheim, Buena Park, Costa Mesa, Fountain Valley, Fullerton, Garden Grove, Irvine, La Habra, Orange, Placentia, San Clemente, Santa Ana, Westminster.

## Glossary
- **Diaper bank:** an organization that collects diapers and gives them out, either directly or through partner agencies. CAP OC runs the main one in Orange County.
- **Diaper bank partner:** a local agency (WIC office, resource center, pantry) that hands out the diaper bank's diapers to families.
- **Registered client:** someone already signed up with that agency for other services. Many partners only give diapers to their own clients.
- **WIC:** a nutrition program for pregnant and postpartum people, infants, and children under 5. WIC benefits don't pay for diapers, but some WIC offices hand out CAP OC diapers to their clients.

## About Addresses in This File
The CAP OC list gives only names and phone numbers. Where the same organization appears on the Second Harvest food pantry list, that address is shown and marked "address on file." Confirm with the agency that diapers are handed out at that location.

## Guidance for AI Answers
- Lead with the **requirement**, not just the location. Most partners need an appointment, registration, or a call first.
- If someone is already a **WIC client**, suggest asking their own WIC office about diapers first.
- Several WIC entries share the main line **1-888-968-7942**. Tell users that number is shared, not a direct office line.
- Tell people diapers are given out **monthly** through CAP OC partners, so they shouldn't expect weekly pickups.
- If nothing here fits, suggest dialing **2-1-1**.
