## How Schedules Are Written

- "Every Tuesday" means weekly.
- "1st & 3rd Saturdays of the month" means only those weeks.
- "5th Saturday" means it only happens in months that have five Saturdays.
- "Irregular schedule" means the source listed it as inconsistent; check the note or call.
