# Food Pantries in Anaheim

**Last verified:** 2026-09-14, from the Second Harvest Food Bank of Orange County partner list. Pantry hours change often, so always call ahead or check with 211 OC (dial 2-1-1).

> [!important] Call before you go
> Hours change often, and some sites serve only residents, enrolled children, or registered clients. Call the site or dial 2-1-1 before making the trip.
>
> **Second Harvest partner sites** (most sites in this file): for the latest schedule and partner list, contact Second Harvest Food Bank of Orange County at **949-653-2900** or **info@feedoc.org**. For school sites, please contact Second Harvest instead of the school.

## Quick Summary

This file lists 58 sites in Anaheim from the Second Harvest partner list: 17 community food pantries, 22 school-site distributions, 8 senior grocery sites, 4 Park-it Market sites, 7 kids meal sites.

- **Days with food distribution (excluding kids meal sites):** Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday, Friday, Saturday, Sunday
- **Weekend options:** ICNA Relief Anaheim, Recovery Road Inc. - Winston, Sarang Community Church, St. Justin Martyr SVDP
- **Evening options (open past 5 PM):** Anaheim High School - The Market at Anaheim PSP, Anaheim United Methodist Church - Neighbors Pantry, ICNA Relief Anaheim, Mariners Church - Anaheim, Recovery Road Inc. - Winston, Western Ave Church of Christ
