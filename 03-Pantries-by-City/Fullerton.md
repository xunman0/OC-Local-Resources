# Food Pantries in Fullerton

**Last verified:** 2026-09-14, from the Second Harvest Food Bank of Orange County partner list. Pantry hours change often, so always call ahead or check with 211 OC (dial 2-1-1).

> [!important] Call before you go
> Hours change often, and some sites serve only residents, enrolled children, or registered clients. Call the site or dial 2-1-1 before making the trip.

## Quick Summary

This file lists 19 sites in Fullerton from the Second Harvest partner list: 10 community food pantries, 7 school-site distributions, 1 senior grocery site, 1 kids meal site.

- **Days with food distribution (excluding kids meal sites):** Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday, Friday, Saturday, Sunday
- **Weekend options:** First Lutheran of Fullerton - Caring Hands FP, Heart of Downtown Food Co-Op, St. Juliana SVDP
- **Evening options (open past 5 PM):** St. Philip Benizi SVDP
