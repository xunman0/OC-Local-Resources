# Food Pantries in San Clemente

**Last verified:** 2026-09-14, from the Second Harvest Food Bank of Orange County partner list. Pantry hours change often, so always call ahead or check with 211 OC (dial 2-1-1).

> [!important] Call before you go
> Hours change often, and some sites serve only residents, enrolled children, or registered clients. Call the site or dial 2-1-1 before making the trip.
>
> **Second Harvest partner sites** (most sites in this file): for the latest schedule and partner list, contact Second Harvest Food Bank of Orange County at **949-653-2900** or **info@feedoc.org**. For school sites, please contact Second Harvest instead of the school.

## Quick Summary

This file lists 7 sites in San Clemente (1 from organization websites or the County Pocket Guide, the rest from the Second Harvest partner list): 3 community food pantries, 3 senior grocery sites, 1 kids meal site.

- **Days with food distribution (excluding kids meal sites):** Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday, Friday, Saturday
- **Weekend options:** COA - Community Outreach Alliance
- **Evening options (open past 5 PM):** None listed
