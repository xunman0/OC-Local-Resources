# Orange County Food Pantry Resources – Overview

## Purpose
This knowledge base helps people in Orange County, California find free or low-cost food. It covers food banks, food pantries, hot meal programs, mobile distributions, and public food benefit programs. It is written for the general public and organized so an AI assistant can retrieve accurate, specific answers.

## Scope
- **Geography:** Orange County, CA only. Statewide or federal programs are included when Orange County residents can use them.
- **Covered:** food pantries, food banks, hot meals, mobile and pop-up pantries, CalFresh, WIC, school meals, senior meal programs, and free diaper resources.
- **Not covered:** housing, utilities, or cash aid, except for brief referrals.

## Rules for Every File
1. **One topic per file.** Each file should answer one kind of question well.
2. **Frontmatter is required.** Every file includes `title`, `type`, `last_verified`, and `sources`.
3. **Date everything that changes.** Hours, locations, and eligibility rules carry a `last_verified` date.
4. **Cite sources.** Link the organization's own page or the 211 OC listing.
5. **Plain language.** Write for someone who may be stressed, new to the system, or reading in a second language.
6. **No guessing.** If a detail is unknown, write "Unknown – call to confirm" instead of leaving it out.

## Guidance for AI Answers
- Recommend that users **call ahead**, because pantry hours change often.
- Recommend **211 OC (dial 2-1-1)** as the backup for current listings.
- For **Second Harvest partner sites**, especially school sites, refer people to **Second Harvest at 949-653-2900** for the latest schedule instead of calling the school.
- Mention the `last_verified` date when giving hours or locations.
- If someone needs food today, point them to **01-How-to-Get-Food-Now** first.

## Glossary
- **Food bank:** a large warehouse organization that collects food and supplies it to many smaller pantries. Most people do not pick up food directly at a food bank.
- **Food pantry:** a local site, often at a church, school, or nonprofit, where people pick up groceries.
- **Mobile pantry:** a truck or pop-up distribution that serves a location on a set schedule.
- **Hot meal program / soup kitchen:** a site that serves prepared meals to eat on site or take away.
- **CalFresh:** California's name for SNAP, the federal food benefit loaded onto an EBT card.
- **WIC:** a nutrition program for pregnant and postpartum people, infants, and children under 5.
- **TEFAP:** The Emergency Food Assistance Program, a federal program that supplies USDA food through food banks. It has its own income eligibility rules.
- **EBT card:** the debit-style card used for CalFresh benefits.
