# Help and Referral Lines

Phone lines and websites that connect people to help. **In an emergency, call 911.**

## Start Here
| Line | Phone | Use it for |
|---|---|---|
| **211 OC** | **2-1-1** (outside OC: 888-600-4357) | Any kind of help: food, shelter, housing, bills, health. 24/7, multilingual. https://www.211oc.org |
| **OC Food Help map** | Online | Free food near you: https://ocfoodhelp.org/orange-county-free-food-map/ |

## Food and Basic Needs
