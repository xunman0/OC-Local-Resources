# CAP OC Diaper Bank Partner List – Merge Notes and Flags

## About This Source
- Read from the CAP OC website on 2026-10-03. The page has no "last updated" date.
- Lists 29 partners with city, name, phone, and notes. **No street addresses.**
- Addresses in the diaper file come from the Second Harvest list where the same organization appears there. Each is marked "address on file."

- [ ] **Cantlay spelling:** "Cantalay" on CAP OC vs. "Cantlay" on the Westminster flyer.
- [ ] **South County Outreach:** listed under Irvine on CAP OC, but the Second Harvest address is in Lake Forest.
