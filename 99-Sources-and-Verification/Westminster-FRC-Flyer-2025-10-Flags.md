# Westminster FRC Food Pantry Flyer (Oct 2025) – Merge Notes and Flags

## About This Source
- The flyer has no printed date. The PDF file was created 10/29/2025, so it is about 11 months older than the Second Harvest partner list (09/14/2026).
- When the two sources disagree, the city files show the Second Harvest hours first and the flyer's version underneath, marked as conflicting.
- The flyer's main added value is **phone numbers** and **requirements** (ID, registration forms), which the Second Harvest list mostly lacks.
- The flyer points readers to the OC Food Help free food map: ocfoodhelp.org/orange-county-free-food-map/

## New Sites Added (not on the Second Harvest list)
- **Blessed Sacrament Church** – Westminster
- **City of Westminster FRC** – Westminster
- **OakView FRC** – Huntington Beach (same address as the Boys & Girls Clubs Oak View kids meal site; may be a separate program)

## Matched by Address, Listed Under a Different Name
- Client Choice Food Pantry = **The Men with Vision Food Pantry** (13552 Goldenwest St, Westminster). Hours match.
- Cantlay Food Distribution Center = **Catholic Charities of Orange County** (2020 W. Chestnut Ave, Santa Ana). Hours match; flyer adds diaper hours.
- City of Stanton FRC = **Stanton City Hall SG** (7800 Katella Ave). Hours match. The flyer doesn't say this is senior-only.
- SOS Community Health Center = **Share Ourselves (S.O.S)** (1550 Superior Ave, Costa Mesa).

- [ ] **Hope Lutheran Church** (Westminster): 1st Friday 9:00 AM–12:00 PM on SHFB vs. every Friday 9:00–11:00 AM on the flyer.
- [ ] **Abrazar** (Midway City): SHFB lists Tue/Thu 11:30 AM–1:30 PM and Fri 12:00–2:00 PM. The flyer lists Tue and Thu only, with numbers handed out early (7:30/8:00 AM) and opening at 10:30/11:00 AM.
- [ ] **Our Redeemer Lutheran Church – Good Roots Market** (Garden Grove): Wednesday 8:00 AM–2:00 PM on SHFB vs. Saturday 8:00–11:00 AM on the flyer.
- [ ] **Refuge Calvary Chapel** (Huntington Beach): flyer starts Mon/Tue at 1:45 PM (SHFB says 2:00 PM) and adds a Thursday food time (11:00 AM–12:00 PM).
- [ ] **Share Ourselves (S.O.S)** (Costa Mesa): SHFB says until 5:00 PM; flyer says until 4:30 PM.
- [ ] **Families Forward** (Irvine): SHFB says Mon 9–2, Tue–Thu 10–3; flyer says Mon–Thu 10–4.
