# How to Get Food Now

For anyone in Orange County who needs food **today or this week**. Lists places that are open on several weekdays, serve hot meals, or give food the same day. Full details for each site are in the city files.

> [!important] Call before you go
> Hours change and food can run out. Call the site, or dial **2-1-1** (24/7, multilingual). For any Second Harvest partner pantry, you can also call **Second Harvest Food Bank at 949-653-2900** for the latest schedule.

## Fastest Ways to Get Help
1. **Dial 2-1-1** (or 888-600-4357). 211 OC can find the closest open pantry or meal right now.
2. **Go to a hot meal site** (below). Most don't require paperwork.
3. **Apply for CalFresh.** If your household has very little income or money, you may get benefits **within 3 days**. See [[CalFresh]].
4. **Check the free food map:** OC Food Help, https://ocfoodhelp.org/orange-county-free-food-map/

## What to Bring
- **Photo ID** (some sites require it; many don't)
- **Proof of address** (some sites limit service to certain cities or ZIP codes)
- **Bags, boxes, or a cart** to carry groceries
- Some sites ask you to **register on your first visit**. Arrive early.
