# Immigration Resources

Immigration legal help, know-your-rights information, and family preparedness resources.

**Status:** copied from source lists dated 2026. Not yet checked with each organization.

> [!important] Call before you go
> Programs, phone numbers, and eligibility rules change. Call the organization or check its website before visiting. For help finding any service in Orange County, dial **2-1-1** (24/7, multilingual).

## Know Your Rights and Family Preparedness

### National Immigration Law Center (NILC) – Everyone Has Certain Basic Rights
- **What they do:** Know-your-rights information for immigrants.
- **Phone:** 213-639-3900
- **Website:** https://www.nilc.org/resources/everyone-has-certain-basic-rights
- **Source:** Legal Resources list (Orange County and surrounding area)

### Immigrant Legal Resource Center (ILRC) – Family Preparedness Plan and Red Cards
- **What they do:** Free guides: a step-by-step family preparedness plan, and "Red Cards" (tarjetas rojas) that explain your rights.
- **Website:** https://www.ilrc.org/red-cards-tarjetas-rojas
- **Notes:** Family preparedness plan: https://www.ilrc.org/resources/step-step-family-preparedness-plan
- **Source:** Legal Resources list (Orange County and surrounding area)

### Comfort Connection Family Resource Center – Immigration Agency List
- **What they do:** Keeps a list of agencies that help with immigration issues. Call to request it.
- **Phone:** 714-558-5400
- **Website:** https://www.rcocdd.com/frc/ccfrc
- **Notes:** This note was attached to the Santa Ana Housing Authority row in the source; moved here.
- **Source:** RCOC community resources spreadsheet (Community Resources 2026)
