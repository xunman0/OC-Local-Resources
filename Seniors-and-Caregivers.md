# Seniors and Caregivers

Aging services, caregiver support, senior health programs, and home-delivered meals.

**Status:** copied from source lists dated 2026. Not yet checked with each organization.

> [!important] Call before you go
> Programs, phone numbers, and eligibility rules change. Call the organization or check its website before visiting. For help finding any service in Orange County, dial **2-1-1** (24/7, multilingual).

## Medicare Counseling

### HICAP (Health Insurance Counseling and Advocacy Program) – Council on Aging Southern California
- **What they do:** Free, unbiased Medicare counseling: understanding benefits, comparing plans, and protecting against fraud.
- **Who it serves:** Serving Seniors and older adults all over California
- **Address:** 2 Executive Circle, Suite 175, Irvine, CA 92614
- **Phone:** 714-560-0424; toll-free 800-434-0222
- **Website:** https://www.coasc.org
- **Notes:** The source description and website were for California Health Advocates (cahealthadvocates.org), a separate statewide Medicare education group. HICAP in Orange County is run by the Council on Aging – Southern California at this address. Phone confirmed on coasc.org (2026-10-04).
- **Source:** RCOC community resources spreadsheet (Community Resources 2026)

## Home-Delivered Meals

### Meals on Wheels (home-delivered meals)
- **What they do:** Meals delivered to the homes of older adults who can't shop or cook, often with a daily wellness check.
- **Who it serves:** Generally adults 60+ who are homebound due to illness, disability, or limited mobility.
- **Phone:** OC Office on Aging (to find your local provider): 800-510-2020
- **Website:** https://officeonaging.oc.gov
- **Notes:** Meals are delivered by local providers, including Meals on Wheels Orange County (https://www.mealsonwheelsoc.org) and Age Well Senior Services (https://agewellseniorservices.org). Some cities, like Irvine, run their own programs. The source listed the national Meals on Wheels America number (888-998-6325). Checked 2026-10-05.
- **Source:** RCOC community resources spreadsheet (Community Resources 2026)

## Caregiver Support

### Family Caregiver Resource Center
- **What they do:** Information and referral, family support groups, caregiver training, for caregivers of adults with brain injury or other impairment.
- **Who it serves:** Caregivers
- **Phone:** 800-543-8312; 714-446-5030
- **Website:** https://www.caregiveroc.org
- **Source:** RCOC community resources spreadsheet (Community Resources 2026)

## Aging Services

### Multipurpose Senior Services Program (MSSP)
- **What they do:** Offers case management, personal care services, respite care, housing assistance, transportation, chores services, protective services, and many more services. CalOptima administers the program.
- **Who it serves:** Age 65 or older, Medi Cal individuals who, but for the provision of such services, would require the Nursing Facility level. Individuals shall only be enrolled in one HCBS waiver at any a time.
- **Phone:** 714-825-3000; 714-347-5780
- **Website:** https://www.dhcs.ca.gov
- **Source:** RCOC community resources spreadsheet (Community Resources 2026)

### Office on Aging (County of Orange)
- **What they do:** Information and referrals on any senior issue, social services, health and medical services, legal services, counseling, social security information, shared housing, employment, nutrition programs and income assistance programs.
- **Who it serves:** Serves Orange County and services are free of charge. For persons age 55 and above.
- **Address:** 1300 S. Grand Ave., Bldg. B, Santa Ana, CA 92705
- **Phone:** 800-510-2020 (fax 714-567-5021)
- **Website:** https://officeonaging.oc.gov
- **Source:** RCOC community resources spreadsheet (Community Resources 2026)

## Senior Health

### Senior Health Outreach Prevention Program (SHOPP)
- **What they do:** County of Orange Health Care Agency community outreach and home visitation program. Offering services such as health screening, behavioral health assessment and evaluation, intervention, linkages, short term case management and consultation/ education. This service is more for the family of the person served rather than the person served.
- **Who it serves:** Everyone
- **Phone:** 714-834-7759
- **Website:** https://www.ochealthinfo.com/shopp
- **Source:** RCOC community resources spreadsheet (Community Resources 2026)

### UCI Health – Senior Health Center
- **What they do:** Assists seniors in the face of age related decline, offers medical and psychiatric services and assists connecting with the best course of treatment for the frail and elderly.
- **Who it serves:** Men and women over age 60 years who need the best course of medical treatment.
- **Phone:** 877-427-7824
- **Website:** https://www.ucihealth.org
- **Source:** RCOC community resources spreadsheet (Community Resources 2026)
