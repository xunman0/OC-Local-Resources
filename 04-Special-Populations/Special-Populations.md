# Help for Specific Groups

A guide to where each group can find food and related help in this knowledge base.

## Families with Babies and Young Children
- **WIC** (pregnant people and children under 5): 888-968-7942. See [[Other-Food-Programs]].
- **Diapers:** CAP OC Diaper Bank partners. See [[Diaper-Resources]].
- **Family shelter and eviction help:** See [[Homelessness-Prevention-for-Families]].
- **Family resource centers:** parenting help, applications, referrals. See [[Family-Resource-Centers]].

## Children and Students
- **Free school breakfast and lunch** for all California public school students. See [[Other-Food-Programs]].
- **Kids meal sites** (marked "KC" in city files): weekday afternoon meals for children 18 and under, usually enrolled in that program.
- **Students without stable housing** keep their school and get transportation (McKinney-Vento). See [[Homelessness-Prevention-for-Families]].
- **Youth ages 11–25:** Orangewood Foundation, Santa Ana. See [[Santa-Ana]].

## Seniors (60+)
- **Senior Grocery sites** (marked "SG" in city files). Call Second Harvest at 949-653-2900 for schedules.
- **Home-delivered meals:** OC Office on Aging, 800-510-2020.
- **Medicare counseling, caregiver support, senior health:** See [[Seniors-and-Caregivers]].

## People Experiencing Homelessness
- **Hot meals, showers, laundry, and mail:** See [[Hot-Meals-and-Mobile]].
- **Ready-to-eat bags** (no kitchen needed): Share Our Selves, Costa Mesa.
- **Shelters:** most adult shelters are by referral. See [[Housing-and-Homelessness]].
- **Outreach:** OC Outreach & Engagement, 800-364-2221.

## Veterans
- **Benefits, housing help, and crisis support:** See [[Veterans-Services]].
- **Veterans Crisis Line:** 988, then press 1.

## People with Disabilities
- **Regional Center, assistive technology, and advocacy:** See [[Disability-Services]].
- **CalFresh for SSI recipients:** See [[CalFresh]].
- **Housing for adults with disabilities:** Grandma's House of Hope. See [[Homelessness-Prevention-for-Families]].

## Immigrants and Mixed-Status Families
- **Eligible children can get CalFresh** even if parents don't qualify. See [[CalFresh]].
- **Most food pantries don't ask about immigration status.** If you are unsure, ask the site before you go.
- **Immigration legal help:** See [[Immigration-Resources]].

## Survivors of Domestic Violence
- **Shelters and hotlines:** Radiant Futures, Human Options, Interval House, Laura's House; National DV Hotline 800-799-7233. See [[Mental-Health-and-Crisis]].
