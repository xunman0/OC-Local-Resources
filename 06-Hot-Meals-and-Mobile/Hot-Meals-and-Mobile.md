# Hot Meals and Mobile Markets

> [!important] Call before you go
> Meal times change and food can run out. Call the site, or dial **2-1-1**. For Park-it Markets, call **Second Harvest at 949-653-2900** for the latest schedule.

## Hot Meals (Ready to Eat)
Most hot meal sites don't require ID or paperwork.

| Site | City | When | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Southwest Community Center (CAP OC) | Santa Ana | Daily 12:00–1:00 PM; Sat–Sun also 8:00–9:00 AM | Meals to go. 714-547-4073 |
| Someone Cares Soup Kitchen | Costa Mesa | Lunch Mon–Fri 12:00–3:00 PM; breakfast Sat–Sun 9:00–11:00 AM | 949-548-8861 |
| The Hub Resource Center | Orange | Hot meals 8:30–10:30 AM and 11:30 AM–1:00 PM | Also laundry, showers, mail. 657-767-5751 |
| First Presbyterian Church of Garden Grove | Garden Grove | Breakfast Tue & Thu 6:30–8:30 AM | Sack lunch and showers until 12:00 PM. 714-534-2269 |

| Refuge Calvary Chapel – Lunch & Prayer | Huntington Beach | Thursday 11:00 AM–1:00 PM | Hot meal, clothes, hygiene products for homeless neighbors |
| First Lutheran Church | Fullerton | Tuesday 6:00–6:30 PM | Hot meal to go. 714-871-7820 |
| St. Paul's Episcopal Church | Tustin | Sunday 4:00–5:30 PM | Sit-down meal |

**Ready-to-eat bags** for people without a kitchen: Share Our Selves, Costa Mesa (Mon–Fri 8:00 AM–4:30 PM). See [[Costa-Mesa]].

**Showers:** The Hub (Orange) and First Presbyterian Church of Garden Grove.

## Park-it Markets (Mobile Markets)
Second Harvest's Park-it Markets bring groceries to a parking lot or community site on a set schedule. Only two are marked **"open site"** in the source list; the others are at housing communities and **may be for residents only**.

| Site | City | When | Open to public? |
|---|---|---|---|
| City of Anaheim – Miraloma Park | Anaheim (2600 E. Miraloma Way) | Every Monday 12:30–1:30 PM | **Yes (open site)** |
| Korean Community Services | Buena Park (7212 Orangethorpe Ave.) | Every Thursday 1:00–2:00 PM | **Yes (open site)** |
| Bel'Age Apartments (Jamboree Housing) | Anaheim (1660 W. Broadway) | Every Monday 10:30–11:30 AM | May be residents only |
| Solara Court Apartments | Anaheim (3335 W. Lincoln Ave.) | Every Tuesday 1:00–2:00 PM | May be residents only |
| Project Access – Malabar | Garden Grove (9777 Bixby Ave.) | Every Friday 10:30–11:30 AM | Call to ask |
| LifeSTEPS – Solaira at Pavilion | Irvine (100 Ridge Valley) | Thursdays (not 5th Thursdays) 10:30–11:30 AM | Call to ask |
| Project Access – Triada | Santa Ana (616 N. Lacy St.) | Every Thursday 1:00–2:00 PM | Call to ask |
| Warwick Apartments (Project Access) | Santa Ana (780 S. Lyon St.) | Every Friday 10:30–11:30 AM | May be residents only |

**El Mercadito at Gilbert High School** (Anaheim) is now a permanent campus market, not a mobile market: Wednesdays 9:00–11:00 AM and 1:30–3:30 PM, by appointment, (714) 999-3738. See [[Anaheim]].
