# WIC, School Meals, and Senior Meals

Food programs for specific groups. For monthly grocery money for any eligible household, see [[CalFresh]]. For food today, see [[01-How-to-Get-Food-Now]].

## WIC (Women, Infants & Children)
- **Who:** Pregnant and postpartum people, and infants and children **under age 5**, with income limits. Families on Medi-Cal or CalFresh often qualify automatically.
- **What:** Healthy foods, formula support, breastfeeding help, and nutrition education.
- **Orange County WIC:** 888-968-7942 · https://www.phfewic.org
- **Diapers:** WIC benefits don't pay for diapers, but several WIC offices hand out CAP OC Diaper Bank diapers to their clients. See [[Diaper-Resources]].

## School Meals
- **All California public school students** can get **free breakfast and lunch** every school day, regardless of family income.
- Families should still fill out the school's meal or income form if asked, because it can help the school get funding and may qualify the family for other benefits.
- **Children without stable housing** have extra school rights. See McKinney-Vento in [[Homelessness-Prevention-for-Families]].
- **After-school meals:** Kids meal sites (marked "KC" in the city files) serve free meals to children 18 and under on weekday afternoons. See [[_Index|Food Pantries by City]].

## Senior Meals
- **Senior Grocery sites** (marked "SG" in the city files): monthly groceries for older adults through Second Harvest. Call **Second Harvest at 949-653-2900** for the latest schedule.
- **Home-delivered meals** (Meals on Wheels): for adults 60+ who are homebound. Call the **OC Office on Aging at 800-510-2020** to find your local provider. See [[Seniors-and-Caregivers]].
- **CalFresh:** Seniors, including people on SSI, can apply. See [[CalFresh]].
